## Hierarchy before decoration

Visible construction should carry meaning: boundary, fill, type, spacing, and motion each explain hierarchy or state. Give the primary heading and task region the clearest emphasis; leave quiet regions unframed.

## Intentional irregularity

Vary span, rhythm, and alignment according to content importance while retaining meaningful source order. A featured story may span two columns; supporting notes should remain smaller and quieter. At 320px, the same content becomes one readable column.

## Restraint is a feature

Avoid equal-loudness card walls, universal heavy borders, random offsets, and a palette that decorates rather than communicates.
