## Project identity wins

A product's identity and explicit brief outrank resemblance to a demo. Then prioritize native semantics and accessibility, Neobrui principles, and finally a recipe's visual suggestion.

## Opt in and remove cleanly

Import the public `neobrui` export once and adopt classes or a scoped role mapping intentionally. Removing the import, classes, and theme scope must preserve semantic HTML, links, controls, and application behavior.

## AI path and verification

The repository-owned guidance lives at `skills/neobrui/SKILL.md`. Use it only for explicit Neobrui or Ryan-design-language briefs. Verify a representative page across light/dark, wide/320px, keyboard focus, unfiltered axe, RTL logical shadows, forced colors, reduced motion, and Chromium/Firefox/WebKit.

Do not claim full accessibility conformance from automation alone; pair evidence with targeted manual review.
